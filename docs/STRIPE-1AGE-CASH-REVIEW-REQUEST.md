**Purpose:** Send through Stripe's official support/contact workflow for the existing 1age account. This document is a request, NOT evidence of approval. The ChatGPT Stripe integration does not provide a support-ticket action.

Subject: Review request — 1AGE Cash closed-loop internal digital credits (1age.cash)

Hello Stripe team,

We are developing 1AGE Cash, a wallet-free, account-based internal credit system for the 1AGE virtual platform operated by 1AGEthe3rd. Our website is https://1age.cash and our broader virtual environment is https://1age.world.

We plan to accept one-time payments from customers using Stripe-hosted Checkout and maintain the resulting credits ourselves in an auditable internal database. Proposed pricing is $1 for 1 Cash credit; the credits would be used only for digital experiences and products directly provided by our own approved operating entity and would not be redeemable for cash.

The initial program would NOT support:
- Customer-to-customer transfers or trading.
- Crypto purchases, swaps, withdrawal, wallets, USDC or the separate $1AGE token.
- Third-party merchant payments, cash-out, payouts or external settlement.
- Gambling, cash-prize games or wagering.

Customers would sign in to a secure account. Payment-confirmed credits would be issued only after server-verified signed Stripe webhooks; approved first-party purchases would debit an internal ledger and grant entitlements. We plan user support, published terms and refund procedures, fraud limits, refund/chargeback reversals and transaction reconciliation.

We recognize Stripe's current Restricted Businesses policy refers to stored value, virtual credits and in-game currency, as well as exceptions and conditions for the operator of a virtual world and seller-issued credits.

Before activating any live payments, could you confirm:
1. Whether this exact 1AGE Cash use case is eligible for payments using the existing 1age Stripe account?
2. Whether an additional restricted-business review or written authorization is required, including any limits on top-ups, balances, refunds or permitted digital goods?
3. Whether purchases across several first-party 1AGE domains operated by one legal entity are allowed, and how to verify that entity relationship?
4. Which checkout/payment methods, risk controls and customer disclosures you require?
5. Whether any parts of the model must be changed to obtain approval?

We are currently testing in Stripe test mode only. We have not enabled live Cash purchases.

Thank you.

1AGEthe3rd Virtual Studio
